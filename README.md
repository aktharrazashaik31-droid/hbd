# For Sai Prasanna | A Wish Written in Light

An immersive, cinematic 3D birthday experience dedicated to **Padala Sai Prasanna**.

Built with **React**, **Three.js**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

---

## The Vision & Narrative Architecture

This project is conceived as a piece of digital installation art and quiet cinematic storytelling. It honors the recipient’s birthday with dignity, restraint, and sincere goodwill:

- **Recipient**: Padala Sai Prasanna
- **Date of Birth**: 15 October 2006
- **Celebration Date**: 16 October
- **Tone**: Reflective, warm, dignified, and sincere. No pressure, no demands, and zero romantic entitlement.

---

## Narrative Scenes

1. **Scene 1 — The Quiet Beginning**: Expansive dark space with solitary light; *"Some people remain in our thoughts, quietly."*
2. **Scene 2 — Her Name**: Elevated typographic staging of **Padala Sai Prasanna** framed within illuminated orbital geometry.
3. **Scene 3 — Her Birthday**: Exact presentation of dates (*Born: 15 October 2006*, *Celebrating: 16 October*).
4. **Scene 4 — A Quiet Truth**: Reflection on unreciprocated goodwill without guilt or blame; *"Not every feeling becomes a shared story. Some can still wish someone well, sincerely and quietly."*
5. **Scene 5 — The Birthday Wish**: Heartfelt, dignified message wishing her peace, health, happiness, and courage.
6. **Scene 6 — The Misunderstanding, Acknowledged with Dignity**: Allowing goodwill to remain untainted without demanding resolution; *"Sometimes things remain unresolved. That does not have to take away the kindness of a sincere wish."*
7. **Scene 7 — The Final Wish & Horizon**: Sculpture radiates expansive light; *"May life bring you beautiful things, Sai Prasanna. Happy Birthday."*

---

## Features & Controls

- **Original 3D Environment**: Procedural concentric orbital rings, luminous faceted core, dynamic starlight, and ascending embers.
- **Cinematic Choreography**: Camera position, focal length, core intensity, and halo scale evolve dynamically across scenes.
- **Atmospheric Audio (Web Audio API)**: Strictly opt-in (disabled by default). Procedurally synthesizes a warm velvet ambient chord pad.
- **Full Accessibility & WebGL Fallback**: If WebGL is unavailable or the user selects "Text View", a high-contrast editorial static layout is displayed.
- **Motion Controls**: Built-in support for `prefers-reduced-motion` and an explicit motion toggle.
- **Controls**: Arrow keys (← / →), Spacebar, on-screen buttons, dots, and mobile touch swipe gestures.
